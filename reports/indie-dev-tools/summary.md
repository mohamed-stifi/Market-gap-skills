# Indie dev and solo-founder tools: results at a glance

**Question.** Which jobs of indie developers, solo founders and micro-SaaS teams (1-5 people) are under-served by current developer and founder software and workarounds, and which could a small SaaS, built mostly by an AI coding agent and run with about 5 human hours a week and a budget under $300, serve profitably (>= $1,000 MRR) with growth built into the product?

Gap ideas that made the ranking: **1 of 12** | Killed by the red team: **2** | Too little evidence: **9** | Verified quotes: **289** | Products and sites mapped: **395** | Landscape search: **Stopped before saturation**

## Verdict

- **1 of 12 gap ideas made the final ranking.** 2 were killed by the red team and 9 had too little evidence.
- **#1 GAP-001: Reliable, uncapped visitor chat for solo founders and tiny online businesses.** Red team: weakened (“The space is not empty; it is saturated with free and cheap supply”). Evidence certainty: pain Low, demand Very low; demand strength weak. Desk score 60.7 of 100 (the only ranked gap, so the weighting test cannot separate it from others). Obtainable revenue: $18,240 a year in the base case (low $216, high $238,032), above your ambition of $12,000 a year.

## Killed by the red team, and why

- **GAP-003 Charging for browser extensions and verifying paid access after Chrome dropped store payments**: 3 existing products already cover it fully (crxpay, ExtensionPay (ExtPay), Addon Pay). The platform withdrawal in 2020-2021 created the gap, and third parties filled it: ExtensionPay (since 2021, multi-browser, trials, subscriptions), Addon Pay, crxpay (entitlements, paywall…
- **GAP-004 Accurate, low-noise outage alerts and public status pages for small software products**: 2 existing products already cover it fully (Pulsetic, OnePageStatus). Free and cheap tools already deliver what the card says is missing: multi-region confirmation before alerting (Pulsetic, OnePageStatus, Hyperping, UptimeRobot), automatic incident updates…

## Near misses (too little evidence)

- **GAP-005 Failed-payment recovery and visibility for small subscription SaaS**: evidence from only 2 websites; 3 needed.
- **GAP-002 VAT and sales-tax filing for small software sellers who sell without a merchant of record**: evidence from only 2 websites; 3 needed.
- **GAP-010 Access to a merchant of record for software sellers rejected or unsupported by providers**: evidence from only 2 websites; 3 needed.
- **GAP-007 Running subscriptions across several payment providers for small software sellers**: evidence from only 2 websites; 3 needed.
- 5 more ideas fell short on several checks: GAP-006, GAP-009, GAP-008, GAP-012, GAP-011.

## Next recommended experiment

- **EXP-01 (problem interviews) for GAP-001**: At least 30% of 40 solo founders using a site chat tool, recruited in indie communities, will report a missed or misrouted visitor message in the last 30 days.
- Stop if: EXP-02 upper confidence bound below 4% paid-checkout starts; EXP-01 upper confidence bound below 30% reporting a missed or misrouted message.

## Caveats

- Landscape search: stopped before saturation (STOP_UNSATURATED). It found at least 171 products in its sampled searches; about 281 are estimated to exist (95% range 236 to 357), so the search is roughly 61% complete (coverage 69%). All such counts are lower bounds.
- Saturation targets were lowered by protocol amendment: completeness from 90% to 50%, coverage from 95% to 50%.
- The protocol was amended 6 times after it was frozen.
- 211 coverage gaps logged (186 pages could not be saved); unavailable sources: gh (not logged in); wiki (MGD_CONTACT not set); directories unit R02-B3-directories (web search denied).
- Coding agreement: provisional (4 codes only tentatively reliable).
- The two largest websites supply 55% of the 289 verified quotes.
- Scores rank desk evidence; demand strength stays weak until validation.
